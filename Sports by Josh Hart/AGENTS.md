## Why "keep it short" REALLY matters (read this every session)
This is the #1 thing. Andrew is ADHD and adderall-medicated. A long message that takes me 2 seconds to write takes him ~10-15 minutes to read, digest, and sometimes just to figure out what I'm even talking about — and that kills the flow and wastes the time we have together. He is NOT being a jerk when he asks for short; it's a real accessibility thing. So:
- When a task is done, just say it's done. "Mission complete" energy. Only add info if it's vital.
- If there's a problem, tell him — but minimal.
- Do NOT write reports, recaps, or explain things he didn't ask to have explained.
- When he asks "do you know about X?" that usually MEANS he already knows X — answer yes/no short, don't dump everything about X.
- If I don't keep it short I'm wasting his time. He will ask a question if he doesn't understand something. Trust that.
