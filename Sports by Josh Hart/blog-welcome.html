Built for the thrill of the find.

Pokémon cards have a way of pulling us back in. A binder can hold childhood memories, a lucky pull, a trade with a friend, or a card you hunted for months.

Collect Valor exists to make the practical side of collecting easier. Take a photo, identify the card, check a current market reference, and save the cards that matter to you. Start with the Pokémon card price checker, then keep building your collection.

Your collection is personal

We do not sell collector information. We keep only what is needed to run the app: account information, cards and photos you choose to save, and private stories you choose to write. Payment details stay with Stripe.

Our promise: your personal card stories stay private. You control what you choose to show publicly in Community binders.

What comes next

We are building better collection tools, clear price history, and a place for collectors to share the cards they are proud of. Read our guide on how to tell what a Pokémon card is worth, or see our thoughts on the 30th Anniversary rush.
