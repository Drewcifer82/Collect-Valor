YOUR DATA, PLAINLY

We don’t sell your data. Here’s exactly what we do.

A card scanner app should respect your collection and your privacy. This page explains what Collect Valor collects, why, and how to delete it — in plain English.

The short version

We collect the least we can, we never sell it, and it's yours to delete. Collect Valor is a card scanner and price checker. We need your email to know you're a member, and we keep the cards you choose to save. That's the heart of it.

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to data brokers or advertisers. The service providers and ad measurement tools below process information to operate the site.
  • We use your email for membership and sign-in codes. Canceling does not automatically delete your account or saved data; email us to request deletion.
  • Your card photos are used to identify the card. They're only stored if you save the card to your collection, and only shown to others if you put that card in your Showcase.
  • You can delete your collection and your account any time by emailing us.

What we never do

  • Sell your email, your collection, or your card photos. Our service providers process the information needed for the features described here.
  • Send marketing email you didn't ask for. If you get an email from us, it's about sign-in, your membership, or a request you sent us.
  • Make your collection public. Only the individual cards you star into your Showcase appear in the Community binders — and you can un-star them any time.
  • Ask for more than we need. The app does not require your real name, address, phone number, or social login. Your optional collector name appears with your Showcase; Stripe may request billing details.

The services that make the scanner work

A card scanner app needs a few partners. These are ours and what each one receives:

  • OpenAI — identifies the card from your photo. We send photos and card-reading instructions through its API with response storage disabled. OpenAI’s own data handling and retention policies also apply.
  • TCG API — provides Pokémon market prices. It receives the card details (Pokémon name, set, number, and printing), not your account email or private back stories.
  • Supabase — securely stores members' saved cards and photos in a private bucket that isn't publicly reachable.
  • Netlify — hosts the site and processes requests, including IP addresses and technical request information.
  • Resend — delivers sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Stripe — processes membership payments.
  • Plausible — cookieless, aggregate site and feature analytics, as described above.

The Journal and outside links

You can read our Journal without signing in or submitting personal information. Journal pages currently do not embed the Plausible analytics script used on other site pages. Netlify still processes page requests to deliver them. There are currently no reader comments or newsletter signup forms.

Articles and card results may link to other sites, including Pokémon, TCGplayer, and eBay. Those sites have their own privacy practices. We do not include your account email or private card stories in those links.

Storage on your device

Your browser stores your sign-in session, watchlist, and install preferences locally. The installed app caches pages and icons. Signing out removes the saved sign-in session. Clearing site data removes local settings and cached content, but does not delete your server-stored collection or cancel your subscription.

Your controls

  • Remove a card: open it in your Collection and tap Remove from collection. The photo is deleted with it.
  • Leave the Showcase: un-star the card. It disappears from the Community binders immediately.
  • Cancel membership: through Stripe, any time. This does not automatically delete your email, account, or collection.
  • Delete everything: email info@collectvalor.com from your membership address and we'll erase your account, your collection, and your photos.
  • Ask us anything: if a question about your data isn't answered here, ask. We'd rather explain than hide it in legalese.

The details

Children. Collect Valor is for collectors 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security. Saved cards and photos live in a private, access-controlled store; all traffic is encrypted; and only our own server functions can read your data. No system is perfect, but we've built this one to hold as little as possible so there's little to lose.

Changes. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page with a new date. We won't quietly start doing something this page says we don't.

Contact. Questions or deletion requests: info@collectvalor.com.
